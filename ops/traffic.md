# Traffic watch

The site runs no ads until traffic qualifies for a premium ad network. Monitor records traffic here weekly and flags in its report when a threshold is crossed. Harry applies; agents never apply to ad networks or change `src/data/site.mjs`.

## Thresholds (as researched October 26, 2026; recheck before applying)

| Network | Reported requirement | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Journey by Mediavine | 1,000 to 10,000 monthly sessions (sources disagree) | https://www.jupiter.co/blog/mediavine-requirements-2026-how-to-qualify , https://newormedia.com/blog/?p=1430 |
| Raptive | 25,000 monthly pageviews; at least 50% from US, UK, CA, AU, NZ below 100,000 | https://www.searchenginejournal.com/raptive-drops-traffic-requirement-by-75-to-25000-views/558780/ |
| Mediavine (main) | Revenue based, about $5,000 a year in ad revenue | https://www.jupiter.co/blog/mediavine-requirements-2026-how-to-qualify |

Both Mediavine and Raptive require Google Analytics and a privacy policy.
