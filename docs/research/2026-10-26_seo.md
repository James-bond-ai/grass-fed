# Research: SEO and competitors

Compiled October 26, 2026. Search volumes come from one free source (seodata.dev, June 2026) and are not verified against a paid tool.

## Competitors and gaps

| Competitor | Strength | Weakness to exploit |
|---|---|---|
| Eatwild | Oldest grass-fed directory, about 622 farms, species and shipping filters | No verification dates, self-attested vetting, little editorial content |
| LocalHarvest | 40,000+ listings, strong city pages | No grass-fed, certification or distance filter; undated |
| AGA and AGW directories | Most credible verification | Weak consumer UX, each covers only its own farms |
| MeatSuite (Cornell, NC State) | Best bulk-buying tools | New York and North Carolina only |
| Affiliate roundups (Fortune, Mamavation) | Authority, recent updates | Grass-fed is one criterion among many; no price-per-lb, grass-finished comparison |

Gaps: filters on grass-finished, certification, bulk shares and shipping; a last-verified date on every listing; grass-fed-only comparisons with price per pound; state-level bulk buying outside NY and NC.

## Keyword clusters

| Cluster | Examples | Winnable for a new domain |
|---|---|---|
| State directory | grass fed beef [state], grass fed beef farms [state] | Yes, top priority, with real listings |
| Bulk buying | buy a quarter cow [state], half cow price [state] | Yes, results are weak |
| City and near me | grass fed beef near me | Partly; city pages only with 5+ farms in reach |
| Delivery comparisons | best grass fed beef delivery, ButcherBox vs Crowd Cow | Medium to hard; win the long tail |
| Dairy and raw milk | raw milk [state], herdshare [state] | Yes for dated, cited state pages |
| Labels | grass fed vs grass finished, AGA vs AGW | Yes |
| Head terms | grass fed vs grain fed | Not within 12 months |

Volumes (US monthly): grass fed beef 33,100; local farm beef 3,600; grass finished beef 2,400; local beef farms 880; grass fed beef farms 590.

## Rules adopted

- Index a state page only at 5 or more verified farms; each state page carries unique data.
- Never create indexable pages per filter combination.
- No self-assigned star ratings in structured data.
- Do not scrape Eatwild or LocalHarvest.
- Google's June 2026 spam update put templated location pages under more scrutiny (https://digitalshiftmedia.com/search-intelligence/google-june-2026-spam-update-home-services/).
