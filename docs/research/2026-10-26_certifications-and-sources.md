## USDA policy on "grass fed"

- AMS withdrew its grass-fed marketing standard effective January 12, 2016. https://nationalaglawcenter.org/usdas-ams-pulls-standard-grass-fed-naturally-raised-meat/
- FSIS 2016 guidance: grass-fed means forage-only after weaning with pasture access in the growing season; substantiated by producer affidavits.
- FSIS December 2019: feedlot beef cannot be labeled grass-fed; percentage claims must describe the diet. https://sustainableagriculture.net/blog/fsis-grassfed-update-label-claim/
- FSIS August 2024 guideline (FSIS-GD-2024-0006) strongly encourages third-party certification for animal-raising claims. Non-binding. https://www.fsis.usda.gov/guidelines/2024-0006
- No federal definition of grass-fed dairy.

Facts such as a farm's name, town and website are not copyrightable (Feist v. Rural Telephone, 1991), but a site's terms can still bind users. Use facts and links only, verify against the farm's own site, and never copy descriptions or photos.

## Compliance

- Supportable with context: grass-fed beef and milk have more omega-3 than conventional (Daley et al. 2010, https://nutritionj.biomedcentral.com/articles/10.1186/1475-2891-9-10 ; Benbrook et al. 2018). Absolute amounts in beef are small: about 0.015 g per 3 oz grass-fed ground beef vs about 1.5 g in salmon (https://www.iowafarmbureau.com/Article/Grassfed-vs-grainfed-beef-Whats-the-healthy-choice).
- Not allowed: disease or health outcome claims; "high in" or "rich in" omega-3; calling a farm certified without evidence; calling grain-finished products grass-fed; unqualified environmental claims (FTC Green Guides).
- FTC Endorsement Guides (16 CFR 255, revised 2023): disclosure clear, conspicuous and near the link; "paid link" is acceptable, "affiliate link" alone is not. https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/ftcs-endorsement-guides-what-people-are-asking
- FTC Consumer Reviews Rule (16 CFR 465, effective October 21, 2024): bans fake or AI-generated reviews and company-controlled review sites presented as independent. https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2024-08-22/html/2024-18519.htm
