Among six national delivery services checked on October 28, 2026, grass-fed ground beef ran from $8.00 to $14.99 a pound before shipping. ButcherBox was the cheapest per pound. Among brands that use the word "grass-finished" (raised on grass and forage until slaughter), a 25 pound bundle from US Wellness Meats had the lowest per-pound price. Shipping can add $20 to $40 to a small order, so the real price depends on how much you buy at once.

## How we compared them

Every service sells cuts at different sizes and fat levels, so we used one common item: a 1 pound pack of grass-fed ground beef at the list price shown on each company's own website. Where a service sells a bulk bundle, we also worked out the per-pound price of that bundle. All prices were read from the companies' websites on October 28, 2026. Prices change often, and sales come and go, so treat these as a snapshot and check the current price before you order.

For each service we also recorded:

- **What it says about feeding.** "Grass-fed" alone does not tell you whether cattle ate grain at the end of their lives. "Grass-finished" or "grass-fed and grass-finished" does. Our guide to [grass-fed vs grass-finished](/guides/grass-fed-vs-grass-finished/) explains the difference.
- **Certifications it names.** We list only what the company names on its own pages. If a page names no certifier, we say so. See [our certifications page](/certifications/) for what each label checks.
- **Where the beef comes from,** when the company says.
- **Shipping costs and free-shipping thresholds.**

Comparisons are based on published information only. We did not order, cook or taste any of these products.

## The services, one by one

### ButcherBox

ButcherBox mainly sells boxes on a subscription. Its FAQ listed three sizes: $179 for up to 21 pounds, $249 for up to 31 pounds and $319 for up to 42 pounds, with shipping free on all boxes. A single 1 pound pack of its 85/15 ground beef was listed at $8.00, the lowest per-pound price we found.

Two things to know. First, ButcherBox sells two kinds of beef: 100% grass-fed beef from Australia and grain-finished beef from the US, according to its FAQ. Products are labeled, so read each listing. Second, the ground beef page says "100% grass-fed" but does not use the word "grass-finished." The FAQ describes the grass-fed line as cattle that eat grass and forage for their whole lives. ButcherBox says all its beef is certified pasture-raised and that its land-based suppliers are certified by Certified Humane or Global Animal Partnership (GAP), which are animal welfare programs rather than grass-fed standards.

Good fit if: you want the lowest per-pound price and are fine with imported beef and a subscription box.

### US Wellness Meats

US Wellness Meats, based in Canton, Missouri, describes all its beef as "100% grass-fed and grass-finished." A 1 pound pack of 85% lean ground beef was $13.98, and a 25 pound bundle was on sale at $297, or $11.88 a pound. That bundle was the cheapest grass-finished option we found per pound.

Shipping is free in the continental US on orders of 15 pounds or more. The company warns that small orders (under 7 pounds) may not arrive frozen, so it pays to order in bulk anyway. It says its cattle farms are in Missouri, Illinois, Alabama, Montana and Tasmania, with some cuts such as skirt and hanger steaks coming from Tasmania. The product page names no third-party certification.

Good fit if: you have freezer space and want the lowest price per pound on grass-finished ground beef.

### Grass Roots Farmers' Cooperative

Grass Roots is a cooperative owned by the farmers who raise the animals, founded in central Arkansas. Its ground beef page says the cattle are "grass-fed and finished" and get no corn, grain or grain by-products, and that every animal is born, raised and harvested in the US. Each package lists its farm of origin.

A 1 pound pack of 80/20 ground beef was $13.95 and a 5 pound pack was $62.75 (about $12.55 a pound), with lower subscription prices. Orders of $149 or more ship free; smaller orders pay $35 per shipment. Its FAQ lists it as a Land to Market verified member, a Savory Institute program that measures land outcomes such as soil and water. The ground beef page itself names no certifier.

Good fit if: you want US-raised, grass-finished beef with the farm named on each pack.

### White Oak Pastures

White Oak Pastures is a family farm in Bluffton, Georgia, that sells by mail. It is also [in our directory](/farm/white-oak-pastures/). Its ground beef page says the beef is "grassfed AND grass finished." A 1 pound pack of 90/10 ground beef was $14.99, and a 25 pound bundle was $374.75, the same $14.99 a pound, or about $13.49 a pound with its 10% subscription discount.

Shipping is $15 on orders over $149 and free over $249, so the 25 pound bundle ships free. The product page names no third-party certification.

Good fit if: you want to buy directly from a single named farm and can order enough to clear the shipping threshold.

### Force of Nature Meats

Force of Nature labels its ground beef "100% grass-fed and grass-finished" and "regeneratively-raised." A 1 pound pack was $14.99. The company says most of its beef comes from family ranches across the US (it lists California, North Carolina, Georgia, Wisconsin, Oregon, Montana and Oklahoma) and that some comes from Land to Market or Regenerative Organic Certified ranches in Australia and New Zealand. The ground beef page does not say which country a given pack comes from.

Shipping is a flat $40 under $189 and free from $189. That $40 fee is the highest we saw, so small orders cost much more per pound.

Good fit if: you want grass-finished beef and other species from one store and will order $189 or more at a time.

### Crowd Cow

Crowd Cow is a marketplace that sells beef from several ranches, both grass-finished and grain-finished. On its 100% grass-fed page, the ground beef available on October 28 was from Certified Piedmontese in Lincoln, Nebraska, at $29.98 for 2 pounds ($14.99 a pound). A grass-fed ground beef from Uruguay was listed at $27.98 for 2 pounds but was sold out.

Because the site mixes grass-finished and grain-finished beef, check the badge on each listing. On the main ground beef page, the Montana ground beef was labeled "Grain Finished." Standard shipping is $19.99 under $149 and free at $149 or more.

Good fit if: you like choosing by ranch and are willing to read each listing's feeding label.

## What a fair price looks like

For context, USDA's Agricultural Marketing Service (AMS) tracks direct-to-consumer grass-fed beef prices. In its April 26, 2024 report, 90% lean bulk ground beef ranged from $8.50 to $14.95 a pound, with an average of $10.98. Ribeye steaks ranged from $25.95 to $40.99, averaging $31.62. That report is two years old and prices have moved since, but it shows that most of the delivery prices above sit at the upper end of what farms charge their direct customers.

Buying a share of a whole animal is often cheaper per pound than any of these services, as long as you have the freezer space. Our guide on [how to buy a quarter cow](/guides/how-to-buy-a-quarter-cow/) walks through the math, and our [bulk beef pages](/bulk-beef/) list farms by state.

## How to read the labels before you order

**Look for "grass-finished."** "Grass-fed" alone can describe cattle that ate grain at the end. Four of the six services use "grass-finished" wording on the ground beef page. ButcherBox and Crowd Cow say "100% grass-fed" instead; ButcherBox's FAQ describes a grass and forage diet for the animal's whole life.

**Check the country of origin.** Several services import some or all of their grass-fed beef. Since January 1, 2026, a "Product of USA" or "Made in the USA" claim on meat may be used only if the animal was born, raised, slaughtered and processed in the US, according to FSIS. The claim is voluntary, so a pack without it is not necessarily imported. If origin matters to you, ask the company.

**Know what a certification covers.** Certified Humane and GAP mostly check animal welfare. Land to Market checks land outcomes. None of these is a grass-fed feeding standard on its own. Our guide to [grass-fed certifications](/guides/grass-fed-certifications-compared/) compares the programs that do check diet.

**Count shipping.** A 2 pound order from Force of Nature at $14.99 a pound plus $40 shipping works out to about $35 a pound. The same beef bought as part of a $189 order ships free. Plan orders around the free-shipping line.

## The bottom line

If price per pound is your main concern, ButcherBox's imported grass-fed beef was the cheapest on the day we checked, and US Wellness Meats' 25 pound bundle was the cheapest beef labeled grass-finished. If you want US-raised, grass-finished beef with a named farm, Grass Roots and White Oak Pastures both state their sourcing clearly. Whichever you pick, confirm the current price and shipping fee on the company's site, read each product's feeding label, and order enough at once to clear the free-shipping threshold.
